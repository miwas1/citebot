01

First run

On the first offline startup, CiteBot automatically queues the bundled sample corpus. It appears in Documents while the worker processes it, then becomes available for search.

$make local-setup

Startup is idempotent: an existing queued, running, or completed bootstrap job is not duplicated.

  1. Provision the local models and start the stack.
  2. Open the workspace at http://127.0.0.1/.
  3. Wait for the sample documents to show Ready.
  4. Ask a question about the sample research papers.
02

Ask the library

Use the Ask workspace for grounded questions. CiteBot retrieves a bounded context, generates a local answer, extracts atomic claims, and checks the citations before returning the response.

Be specificName the document, timeframe, or comparison you care about.
Inspect citationsSelect a numbered source marker to open its supporting passage.
Trust the statusQualified or insufficient answers need more evidence; contradictions are surfaced rather than hidden.
03

Documents & versions

Open Documents to upload PDF, Word, text, Markdown, JSON, JSONL, or image sources. In queued mode, the document worker processes uploads without blocking the API.

Each re-upload can become an immutable version with source anchors, structured extraction metadata, and current-version filtering. The backend also supports exact diffs between versions for change reports.

GET /api/v1/documentsGET /api/v1/documents/{id}/versions
04

Workflows & review

Workflow packs turn research into structured work products for contract review, compliance mapping, vendor comparison, due diligence, and investigation timelines.

High-stakes products pause at a durable review checkpoint. Reviewers can inspect findings, edit permitted values, approve, reject, request reanalysis, or export an evidence bundle.

GET /api/v1/workflowsGET /api/v1/workflows/reviewsGET /api/v1/workflows/{id}/export
05

Operate locally

Offline mode keeps documents, embeddings, generation, vectors, sessions, and evaluation on the local stack. Hosted models and web search are disabled by policy.

StorageBack up storage/ and the model manifest. Qdrant vectors can be rebuilt.
KeysSet separate research and admin keys for a private server; enter them from the workspace gear menu.
CapacityKeep at least 2 GB free on the 16 GB target host and avoid concurrent OCR and deep review.

The full deployment and backup runbook is available in the repository at docs/private-server-deployment.md.
